FAQ

Questions

How accurate is the measurement?

Jumpmaxx uses flight time, the same method as jump mats. It needs a clear view of your whole body and good light. Results are most accurate on newer iPhones with a high frame rate.

Do I need any equipment?

No. To test your jump you only need your iPhone and some floor space. Some workouts use a box, weights or a band, and the app shows you what each one needs.

What does it cost?

Jumpmaxx is a subscription, monthly or yearly. You can see the price in your currency in the app before you buy. You can cancel any time in your Apple ID settings.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. The video is analysed on your phone and deleted when you're done. Only your results, like height and score, are saved on your phone.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone only for now.
