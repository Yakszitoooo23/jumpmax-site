Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The short version
  • Your jump videos are analysed on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • Your results, workouts and progress are stored only on your phone.
  • We have no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment and RevenueCat checks your subscription.

1. Who we are

JumpMax ("the app", "we", "us") is made by Yakup Bjork Onurlu, Denmark. We are the data controller for the app. You can contact us at jumpmaxx@proton.me.

2. Camera and jump videos

The app uses your camera to film your jumps. If you choose, it can also open one video from your photo library.

  • The video is analysed on your iPhone with Apple's on-device body tracking. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Videos are kept only while you look at the result, and are deleted when you leave it. An imported video is a temporary copy. The original stays in your photo library untouched.
  • The app does not use your microphone and does not record sound.

3. Data stored on your phone

To show your progress, the app saves the following on your iPhone:

  • Your onboarding answers (for example your level and goals), used to choose your training program
  • Jump results: height, Jump Score, form scores, date and time
  • Workouts and sets you log, XP, level, streak and reminder settings
  • A random install ID that is not linked to your name

We cannot see this data. It may be included in your iPhone or iCloud backup if you have backups turned on, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. If you delete the app, this data is deleted from your phone.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your name, card or Apple ID details.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether your subscription is active and to restore purchases. For this, RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous app user ID
  • Your purchase and subscription history from the App Store
  • Basic technical data such as device model, iOS version, app version, country and IP address

RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf. RevenueCat is based in the USA, and the transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

5. Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules training reminders on your phone. They are created on your device. We don't send push notifications from a server.

6. What we don't do

  • No user accounts or logins
  • No advertising, and no ad or analytics trackers
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

7. Legal basis (GDPR)

Camera, video and progress data are processed only on your phone, and we never receive them.

8. How long we keep data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or delete the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal accounting rules. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data. You can also object to or restrict processing, and ask for a copy of your data. Email us at jumpmaxx@proton.me and we will answer within one month. To delete all data on your phone, simply delete the app.

You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) at datatilsynet.dk or to the authority where you live.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top. Big changes will also be shown in the app.
