How do I film a good jump?

  • Lean your iPhone against something on the floor with the screen facing you.
  • Step back until your whole body, from head to feet, is on screen.
  • Use good light and stand in front of a plain background if you can.
  • Wear shoes and jump on a hard, flat surface.

My jump height looks wrong

The height is worked out from how long you are in the air. Tucking your knees or landing on your heels can make it look higher or lower. Land on the balls of your feet with straight legs, in the same spot you took off from. Make sure your feet are visible the whole time.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open the app, go to the paywall or settings and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose JumpMax. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

All your data is stored on your phone. Delete the app to delete it. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Safety

JumpMax gives general training information, not medical advice. Warm up before you jump, talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, and stop if you feel pain.
